Welcome to PolGentas

PolGentas is a productivity and task management application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining your trust. PolGentas follows a Local-First architecture — your data is stored locally on your device by default, and we do not collect, upload, or share your personal information.

🔒 Our Core Promise: No data collection. No tracking. No ads. No third-party analytics. Everything you create and manage within PolGentas stays on your device unless you explicitly choose otherwise.

1. Information We Collect

PolGentas does not collect any personally identifiable information. We do not require an account, and we do not have access to any data you create or store within the app. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • 📁 Your tasks, projects, notes, or any user-generated content
  • 📅 Your schedules, deadlines, or calendar data
  • 🏷️ Your labels, categories, or organizational preferences
  • 📊 Usage statistics, analytics, or crash logs
  • 📍 Location data, contacts, or device identifiers

The only exception is when you voluntarily contact us via email — your email address is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is never shared or used for marketing purposes.

2. Local Storage & Deletion

All data in PolGentas is stored exclusively on your device (e.g., within the app sandbox directory). The app does not connect to any cloud servers, does not sync data across devices, and does not transmit any information over the network.

🗑️ Uninstall = Permanent Deletion: If you uninstall PolGentas, all locally stored data — including tasks, projects, settings, and preferences — will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Please export or manually back up any important information before uninstalling.

We cannot access your data, and therefore cannot assist in recovering deleted content. We strongly recommend that you regularly back up your device.

3. Device Permissions

PolGentas does not require any sensitive permissions (such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage access beyond its own sandbox). The app operates entirely offline and only uses basic local storage capabilities that are managed automatically by the operating system.

  • 📁 Local storage: Used solely to save app data within the app's private directory.
  • 🌐 Network access: Not used. PolGentas is a fully offline application.

If future versions require additional permissions, we will update this policy and notify you prominently within the app.

4. Third-Party Services

PolGentas does not integrate any third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or crash reporting frameworks. The app is completely self-contained and does not communicate with any external servers or services.

This privacy policy applies solely to PolGentas and does not cover the privacy practices of any other applications or services on your device.

5. Data Security

Since your data resides only on your device, its security depends primarily on your device's protective measures. We recommend that you:

  • 🔐 Enable a strong passcode, PIN, or biometric authentication on your device.
  • 🔄 Keep your operating system and apps updated with the latest security patches.
  • ⚠️ Avoid using PolGentas on shared or public devices.
  • 📤 Be cautious when transferring device backups — ensure they are stored securely.

PolGentas has no network functionality, so there is no risk of data interception during transmission.

6. Children's Privacy

PolGentas is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app collects no data whatsoever, there is no risk of children's data being collected.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If significant changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide a notice within the app. Since PolGentas is an offline application, we encourage you to periodically review this page.

Your continued use of PolGentas after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Your Rights & Contact Us

You have the right to:

  • ✔️ Use PolGentas completely anonymously — no account, no tracking.
  • ✔️ View, modify, or delete all data within the app at any time.
  • ✔️ Completely erase all data by uninstalling the app.
  • ✔️ Contact us with any privacy questions or concerns.
